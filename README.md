## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, cultural heritage authorities, and traveler assistance networks.

- [Peru Travel (Official Tourism Portal - PROMPERÚ)](https://www.peru.travel/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination guides, cultural itineraries, and regional highlights.
- [iPeru Traveler Assistance Service](https://www.peru.travel/useful-data/iperu) - Official 24/7 tourist guidance, information counseling, and consumer support network operated by PROMPERÚ.
- [SERNANP Protected Natural Areas Portal](https://visitaareasnaturales.sernanp.gob.pe/en/) - Official government administration managing national parks, reserve entry rules, and trail permits across Peru.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating scenic mountain railways, luxury train lines, domestic aviation, and long-distance buses.

- [PeruRail](https://www.perurail.com/) - Primary railway operator offering scenic passenger trains (Vistadome, Hiram Bingham) connecting Cusco, Sacred Valley, and Machu Picchu (Aguas Calientes), plus routes to Puno.
- [Inca Rail](https://incarail.com/) - Luxury and tourist train service operating daily rail connections through the Urubamba River valley to Machu Picchu.
- [LATAM Airlines Peru](https://www.latamairlines.com/pe/es) - National flag carrier operating high-frequency domestic flights linking Lima with Cusco, Arequipa, Iquitos, and Puerto Maldonado.
- [Cruz del Sur](https://www.cruzdelsur.com.pe/) - Major long-distance coach network providing comfortable intercity bus connections across Peru's coastal and Andean highways.

## Destinations & Regions

### Lima & Central Coast

- [Visit Lima (Official Municipal Portal)](https://www.peru.travel/) - Visitor guide for exploring Lima's historic Centro Histórico, world-renowned culinary districts (Miraflores, Barranco), and Larco Museum.
- [Paracas National Reserve Portal](https://visitaareasnaturales.sernanp.gob.pe/en/) - Official guide for coastal desert landscapes, marine wildlife boat tours to the Ballestas Islands, and red sand beaches.

### Cusco, Sacred Valley & Machu Picchu

- [Machu Picchu Official Booking Portal (Ministry of Culture)](https://www.gob.pe/cultura) - Official government ticketing platform for securing mandatory advance entry passes to the Inca citadel.
- [Cusco Regional Tourism Hub](https://www.peru.travel/) - Official guide for exploring imperial Inca stone architecture, Sacsayhuamán, and Sacred Valley artisan markets.

### Arequipa & Colca Canyon

- [Arequipa Tourism Portal](https://www.peru.travel/) - Visitor guide for the "White City" built from volcanic sillar stone, Santa Catalina Monastery, and surrounding snow-capped volcano peaks.
- [Colca Canyon Official Visitor Guide](https://www.peru.travel/) - Regional portal for spotting Andean condors, terraced agricultural valleys, and high-altitude trekking routes.

### Puno & Lake Titicaca

- [Lake Titicaca Tourism Guide](https://www.peru.travel/) - Official travel hub for exploring the highest navigable lake in the world, floating Uros reed islands, and Taquile community textiles.

### Amazon & Northern Routes

- [Manu National Park Portal](https://visitaareasnaturales.sernanp.gob.pe/en/anps/manu-national-park/) - Official administration guide for exploring unbroken cloud forests, oxbow lakes, and premier biodiversity reserves in the Amazon basin.
- [Iquitos Amazon River Cruises](https://www.peru.travel/) - Visitor portal for booking expedition riverboats and exploring Pacaya-Samiria National Reserve.

## National Parks & Conservation

Resources for exploring Peru's protected natural areas, biosphere reserves, and responsible tourism guidelines.

- [SERNANP Official PNA Directory](https://visitaareasnaturales.sernanp.gob.pe/en/) - Comprehensive registry of Peru's 76 nationally protected natural areas, national parks, and wildlife sanctuaries.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on altitude sickness (soroche) prevention, emergency contacts, and tourist police support.

- [Tourist Police Peru (POLTUR)](https://www.peru.travel/) - Specialized police division providing safety patrols, security assistance, and guidance for international visitors in major hubs.
- [General Emergency Hotline (Police 105 / Medical 106)](https://www.gob.pe/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all regions.
